# Question 3

## Problem
A number of minor automobile accidents occur at various high-risk intersections in Teton County despite traffic lights. The Traffic Department claims that a modification in the type of light will reduce these accidents. The county commissioners have agreed to a proposed experiment. Eight intersections were chosen at random, and the lights at those intersections were modified. The numbers of minor accidents during a six-month period before and after the modifications were:

| Number of Accidents | A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 5 | 7 | 6 | 4 | 8 | 9 | 8 | 10 |
| After  | 3 | 7 | 7 | 0 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 2 |

At the 0.05 level of significance, is there evidence that the modification in the type of light will reduce the accidents?

## Solution

**Differences** ($d = \text{Before} - \text{After}$)

$$d_1 = 5 - 3 = 2,\ d_2 = 0,\ d_3 = -1,\ d_4 = 4,\ d_5 = 4,\ d_6 = 3,\ d_7 = 0,\ d_8 = 8$$

Mean difference:

$$\bar{d} = \frac{d_1 + d_2 + \cdots + d_8}{n} = \frac{2 + 0 + \cdots + 8}{8} = \frac{20}{8} = 2.5$$

Standard deviation of the differences:

$$S_d = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(d_i - \bar{d})^2}{n-1}} = \sqrt{\frac{(2-2.5)^2 + \cdots + (8-2.5)^2}{7}} = \sqrt{\frac{60}{7}} \approx 2.928$$

**1. Hypotheses**

$$H_0: \mu_d = 0 \quad \text{versus} \quad H_a: \mu_d \ne 0$$

**2. Significance level**

$$\alpha = 0.05$$

**3. Test type**

This is a paired *t* test, with $df = n - 1 = 8 - 1 = 7$.

**4. Critical value**

For $\alpha = 0.05$, the critical *t* value is $\pm 2.365$.

**5. Test statistic**

$$t = \frac{\bar{d} - \mu_d}{S_d/\sqrt{n}} = \frac{2.5 - 0}{2.928/\sqrt{8}} = 2.415$$

**6. Decision and conclusion**

Since $t = 2.415 > 2.365$, reject $H_0$ at the 0.05 level.

There is evidence that the modification in the type of light will reduce the accidents.
